# Ian Andwati

## Some things about me:

- I'm a software engineer by day. Cyber Security is my hobby and my side work.
- I have a degree in computer science, and most of what I do for fun still centres on it.
- I'm the captain of the [**binarysith**](https://ctftime.org/team/379200) CTF team. I play CTFs on the weekends under the alias **mockingspectre**.
- I like infrastructure-level software: the code that other code runs on.
- I write essays on software development, from personal experience to opinion pieces (with varying degrees of "success"). The blog has been going since 2021 and has moved around a lot: different domains, Medium, dev.to, Hashnode. This site is where I keep the originals.
